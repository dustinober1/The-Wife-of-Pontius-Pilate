## Passion Week control

The outline must preserve a causal sequence rather than devotional pageant:

1. Jesus enters separate intelligence streams before the feast.
2. Roman concern focuses on crowd and title claims; Temple concern on authority, order, and religious danger; Herodian concern on Galilean jurisdiction and rivalry; household reports emphasize lived hope and rumor.
3. The city’s density and jurisdictional ambiguity narrow options.
4. Arrest and preliminary hearings occur off Claudia’s direct view.
5. Pontius receives a politically shaped case.
6. Herod’s role follows Luke as a disputed but adopted narrative element.
7. The Prologue warning enters before final judgment.
8. Handwashing and release custom are biblically attested but historically disputed and must not be narrated as neutral administrative routine.

## Geography and terminology locks

- Caesarea is the normal prefectural base; Jerusalem residence for the feast is a dramatic placement in Herod’s former palace complex.
- The exact praetorium and tribunal location are disputed. “Lithostrotos/Gabbatha” and the forty-yard terrace line are the novel’s chosen geography, not established archaeology.
- Use **bema** for the judgment platform where context supports it; do not treat every courtyard or hearing as the same space.
- Pontius is prefect, not procurator.

## Continuing checks

Keep the standards before the aqueduct; the aqueduct well before Sejanus’s fall; John’s death uncertain in date and motive; Jesus reports before Passion Week; Gerizim before recall; Caiaphas’s removal after Pontius’s recall order; Tiberius’s death before Pontius arrives.
