Revise in manuscript order unless a batch note explicitly requires a cross-chapter control check. Do not draft Chapter 18 until all five batches are accepted and the Chapter 18 lock is revalidated.

## Batch 1 — Foundation and Claudia’s tools

**Chapters:** Prologue, 1–4.  
**Dependencies:** governing premise, Claudia access model, Pontius creed, tribunal geography classification.  
**Principal objective:** reduce prophetic certainty and establish Claudia’s concrete access to petitions, accounts, correspondence, household diplomacy, Marcus, and Tamar.  
**Continuity risks:** seven-year marriage; c. A.D. 18 appointment talk; prefect title; Sejanus sponsorship uncertainty; Prologue note route.  
**Agency gains:** Claudia negotiates access, performs an early information task, and reverses one household decision.  
**Thriller gains:** chapters end with access, order, warning, or consequence rather than theme.  
**Historical checks:** appointment, Caesarea base, Caiaphas/Annas roles, tribunal geography.  
**Acceptance:** every chapter has objective/opposition/information change; no dream-as-proof; no manuscript event moved.  
**Recheck:** OUTLINE, CHARACTERS, STYLE, and Prologue continuity.

## Batch acceptance procedure

For each batch:

1. Run `python scripts/word_count.py` before and after.
2. Compare changed files against the batch scope.
3. Verify no later chapter prose changed.
4. Check each chapter against its mission lock.
5. Update control documents only for genuinely new locked facts.
6. Record word-count delta, continuity decisions, unresolved uncertainties, and acceptance verdict.
