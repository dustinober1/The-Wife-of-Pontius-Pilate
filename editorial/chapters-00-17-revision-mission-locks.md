# Prologue and Chapters 1–17 Revision Mission Locks

These locks govern prose revision. They do not authorize drafting Chapter 18 or changing historical outcomes. Global locks: close third; no Jesus or John POV; no em dashes; no single conspiracy; Claudia uses household-scale access and can be wrong; preserve documented sequence and classify uncertainty through the register.

## 00 Prologue — The Shadow of the Bema
- **Function/verdict:** strongest thriller mechanism; significant revision.
- **Purpose/objective:** Claudia must get a warning into the tribunal before judgment while knowing less than the current narration claims.
- **Pressure/agency:** time, distance, decorum, chain of custody; she selects Marcus and the legal scribe and accepts household risk.
- **Information/conflict:** dream plus partial prior reports and direct observation; Pontius’s political fear is inferred, not omniscient fact.
- **Causal link:** becomes payoff for Claudia’s years of source comparison and selective disclosure.
- **Historical/continuity locks:** Matthew-derived note; Marcus-to-scribe-to-Pontius route; chosen terrace/Lithostrotos geography labeled dramatic; Jesus external only.
- **Prose risks:** devotional certainty, “terrifyingly pure,” explained symbolism, prophetic summary.
- **Preserve:** delivery mechanics, locked gaze, urgency.
- **Prohibit:** dream as proof, new tribunal result, Jesus interiority.
- **Target:** 1,500–1,900.
- **Ending state:** warning reaches Pontius; choice remains open.

## 01 The Red Seal
- **Function/verdict:** governing premise and marriage engine; significant revision.
- **Purpose/objective:** Claudia secures concrete advisory access before accepting the journey.
- **Pressure/agency:** Pontius’s ambition, Sejanus dependence, marital hierarchy; she negotiates petitions, accounts, selected correspondence, and diplomatic-hostess access.
- **Information/conflict:** appointment and patronage are politically useful but historically uncertain; she sees Judaea as a cultural problem Pontius reads as administration.
- **Causal link:** creates tools later used in standards, aqueduct, and Jesus-report chapters.
- **Locks:** prefect title; Sejanus role framed as Pontius’s belief/inference; seven-year marriage and departure continuity.
- **Risks:** prophecy, explained water/map symbolism, polished thesis dialogue, “not X but Y.”
- **Preserve:** map scene, Pontius’s creed, intimacy and ambition.
- **Prohibit:** Claudia obtaining official authority.
- **Target:** 2,700–3,100.
- **Ending:** she goes with defined access and an unresolved concern.

## 02 The Roman Bride
- **Function/verdict:** character foundation; light-to-significant revision.
- **Purpose/objective:** show how usefulness becomes political judgment rather than passive conditioning.
- **Pressure/agency:** guardian power and arranged marriage; Claudia demands truth and performs an early information task for Pontius.
- **Information/conflict:** his fear of being forgotten; patronage talk remains vague eastern-posting speculation.

- **Locks:** ward background, ink motif, c. A.D. 18 meeting, A.D. 19 marriage.

- **Prohibit:** specific foreknowledge that Gratus will leave in A.D. 26.
- **Target:** 1,700–2,100.
- **Ending:** the c. A.D. 24 return yields a concrete shared task or is deliberately de-emphasized.

## Acceptance gate for every revised chapter

The chapter must preserve continuity, contain a measurable objective and opposition, change the tactical or information state, end in a consequence, follow the historical register, and avoid replacing institutional complexity with conspiracy or devotional certainty.
