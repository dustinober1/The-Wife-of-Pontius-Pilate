## Corrections made

### Chapter 6

The ending previously forecast that Pontius would use specific habits in an unidentified future crisis and stated that he did not yet know which problem would require them. The audit replaces that retrospective foretelling with an immediate act: Pontius dictates and seals standing instructions while the standards protesters are still leaving.

The revised ending keeps the wrong lesson concrete:

- shorten the interval before opposition can gather;
- identify roads, provisions, messengers, and gathering places;
- hold soldiers from view until force can complete its purpose;
- send a written account with the order;
- establish technical authority before a public hearing can force visible retreat.

This now fixes the later aqueduct and Gerizim habit through present action instead of authorial forecast.

### Epilogue

The Batch 9 draft correctly fixed Tiberius's death on 16 March A.D. 37 but rendered that date as the sixteenth day before the Kalends of April. Under inclusive Roman counting, 16 March is the seventeenth day before the Kalends of April. The audit corrects the Roman form and labels the A.D. date as a later reckoning, avoiding an anachronistic claim that Claudia used the later era system.
