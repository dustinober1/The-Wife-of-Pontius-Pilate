## Class 2: elapsed-time arithmetic

Three errors, all running low against the `TIMELINE.md` anchors.

- `10-blood-in-the-colonnade.md`: "across seven years of marriage" corrected to **ten**. Marriage is A.D. 19 and the aqueduct riot is A.D. 29. Seven was the figure for A.D. 26, the year of arrival.
- `12-herodiass-court.md`: "three years" in the province corrected to **four**, in both the dialogue and the narration. `TIMELINE.md` places the Herodian visit at c. A.D. 30–31, and Chapter 13 confirms it by dating John's death three years after the river preaching of A.D. 28.
- `15-news-from-the-palatine.md`: the chapter contradicted itself. Two lines said four completed years; one said three completed years and "the fourth year of my administration." Standardized to **four completed years, now the fifth**, which matches the A.D. 31 joint consulship.

One further instance in the same class was found during correction and fixed: `16-the-fall-of-sejanus.md` read "across four years" for Claudia's accumulated channel headings at a point roughly nine months after Chapter 15. Corrected to **five**.

Verified as already correct and left unchanged: Chapter 2's childhood scene, "eight years later," and "five years into the marriage"; Chapter 8's "two years"; Chapter 10's "three years into the practice"; Chapter 13's "three years earlier"; Chapter 27's "three years"; Chapter 29's "ten years of office."
